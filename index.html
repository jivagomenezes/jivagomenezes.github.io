Based in Lisbon, my journey moved from the analytical precision of data discovery to the architectural rigour of Data Engineering at KLx/AZQORE. I see infrastructure as a craft — it must be robust enough to support global financial scale, yet agile enough to power the AI revolution.

My expertise sits at the intersection of traditional data plumbing and modern AI workflows. While many focus on the models, I build the Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) foundations and high-throughput pipelines that make Large Language Models reliable and scalable in production. Most AI engineers haven't spent years debugging Kafka consumer lag at 3am in a regulated banking environment. I have.

Beyond the terminal, I believe the mindset that solves complex engineering problems is the same one that thrives on the tennis court or decodes geopolitical shifts. I'm a student of systems — whether digital, economic, or strategic — and that curiosity makes me a better engineer.
